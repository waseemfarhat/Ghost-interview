Abstract 
 
AI Interview Ghost (Mock Interviewer) is an intelligent web -based interview preparation 
system designed to simulate real -time job interviews using artificial intelligence. The system 
provides users with an interactive interview experience through a virtual AI avatar that asks 
domain-specific interview questions using a realistic human -like voice. Unlike traditional 
mock interview systems, this project focuses not only on the verbal responses of the user but 
also on non -verbal cues such as facial expressions, eye contact, confidence level, and 
emotional state. 
The proposed system analyzes the candidate’s speech, emotions, and body lang uage using 
advanced AI models and generates a comprehensive Confidence and Competency Report. 
This report highlights communication skills, nervousness indicators, usage of filler words, and 
overall interview readiness. The system aims to help students and job seekers improve their 
interview performance through data -driven feedback and self-assessment.

1. Introduction 
This document presents the project scope for the proposed system titled AI Interview Ghost 
(Mock Interviewer) . The purpose  of this project is to provide an intelligent and interactive 
interview preparation platform using artificial intelligence technologies. Interviews are a 
critical part of job selection processes, and many candidates struggle due to lack of practice 
and real-time feedback. Traditional mock interviews are limited in availability and often lack 
detailed performance analysis.  
With the advancement of AI, it is now possible to simulate realistic interview scenarios. This 
system integrates voice interaction, facial emotion detection, and speech analysis to create a 
virtual interviewer experience. The AI avatar interacts with users through voice -based 
questions and evaluates their responses using multiple AI models. The project is developed 
as a web -based applicatio n to ensure accessibility and scalability. The system is particularly 
useful for students, fresh graduates, and professionals preparing for technical or HR 
interviews. 
2. Problem Statement 
Many job candidates face difficulty during interviews due to nervousne ss, lack of confidence, 
and poor communication skills. Most available interview preparation platforms focus only on 
question-answer practice and ignore non -verbal communication aspects such as facial 
expressions, eye contact, and emotional stability. Human  mock interviews are costly, time -
limited, and not always available to everyone.  
Existing systems also fail to provide real -time emotion analysis and speech behavior insights 
such as filler word usage. Candidates do not receive objective feedback on their confidence 
and emotional performance. There is a lack of intelligent systems that combine voice 
interaction, emotion detection, and AI -driven evaluation in a single platform. This project is 
developed to overcome these limitations and to provide a comprehe nsive interview 
preparation solution while enhancing the students’ skills in artificial intelligence, machine 
learning, and web technologies.  
3. Problem Solution for Proposed System 
The proposed AI Interview Ghost system addresses the identified problems by p roviding a 
fully automated mock interview environment. The system allows users to select a job role, 
after which an AI avatar conducts an interview using voice -based interaction. The user 
responds through a webcam and microphone, enabling the system to analyze both verbal and 
non-verbal behavior. 
 3 
Speech-to-text conversion is used to analyze spoken responses and detect filler words. 
Emotion detection models analyze facial expressions and eye movement to determine 
nervousness and confidence levels. Sentiment analysis is applied to evaluate the emotional 
tone of the answers. Based on these analyses, the system generates a detailed Confidence 
and Competency Report. This solution provides users with actionable insights and helps them 
improve interview performance  through repeated practice.

6. Scope 
The scope of AI Interview Ghost includes developing a web-based interview simulation system 
using AI technologies. The system will allow users to select job roles and participate in AI -
driven interviews. The AI avatar will ask interview questions using voice interaction. The 
system will analyze user responses through speech -to-text processing and facial emotion 
detection. A confidence and c ompetency report will be generated after each interview 
session. 
The project includes sentiment analysis, filler word detection, and emotion recognition. The 
system will not provide real job placements or human interview scheduling. Advanced AI 
model train ing is out of scope and pre -trained models will be used. The focus remains on 
interview preparation and performance analysis.  
7. Modules 
Module 1: User Role Selection Module 
Allows users to select job roles and interview categories.  
Module 2: AI Interview Avatar Module 
Conducts interviews using voice-based AI avatar. 
Module 3: Speech Analysis Module 
Converts speech to text and detects filler words.  
Module 4: Emotion & Sentiment Analysis Module 
Analyzes facial expressions and emotional tone.  
Module 5: Confidence & Competency Report Module 
Generates performance reports with insights.  
Module 6: System Dashboard Module 
Displays interview history and analytics.
